<details><summary>विस्तारः (द्रष्टुं नोद्यम्)</summary>

This video features a scholarly discussion on the core differences between the **Madhva (Dvaita)** and **Ramanuja (Vishishtadvaita)** schools of Vedanta, focusing on the concept of *Sharira-Shariri Bhava* (the relationship between the body and the soul/controller).

* **The Madhva View (Speaker 1):** Argues that God (Narayana) possesses a completely transcendental body made of pure knowledge and bliss (*Jnana-Anandatmaka*). Material nature (*Prakriti*) and flawed individual souls (*Jivas*) can never be considered God's body. God pervades everything as the inner controller (*Antaryami*) but remains entirely distinct from material creation. He is strictly the efficient cause (*Nimitta Karana*), not the material cause (*Upadana Karana*).
* **The Ramanuja View (Speaker 2):** Argues that according to the scriptural texts (*Shrutis*), there exists a literal *Sharira-Shariri Bhava* where the universe and the souls constitute the "body" of the Supreme Lord, who is the soul. They propose *Visheshana Parinamavada* (transformation of God's attributes/body) rather than *Svaroopa Parinamavada* (transformation of His essential nature), suggesting the difference between the two schools on this point is largely a matter of terminology rather than core intent.
